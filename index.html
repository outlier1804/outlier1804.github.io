Small, finished tools. Each one is free, open source, and runs without an account — most of them run entirely in your own browser or on your own machine.

ClipGate — check a clip against a campaign brief before you submit it

Paste a paid-clipping or UGC campaign brief and your clip's details. ClipGate turns the brief into a rule list, checks what it can automatically, and tells you which rules still need your own eyes. No upload, no backend — it runs in the browser tab.

Quote Drafter — job notes to a priced estimate

For trades. You describe the job in plain language against your own rate card; it does every number in exact decimal arithmetic — markup, call-out minimum, after-hours, tax, rounding — and refuses to invent a price for a service code it doesn't know. Python 3, standard library only.

Agent Workspace Kit — the plumbing behind a self-running agent

The extracted, working parts of a Claude Code agent that runs unattended: a Telegram bridge, a markdown-vault memory, scheduled headless workers, subagents, and a local dashboard. It is the scaffolding, not a demo — the pieces that are tedious to rebuild.
